Service limitations
How AgilePredict handles traffic, caching and automated access

AgilePredict is a free, hobby-scale service running on a small single instance. To keep it responsive for everyone, a few limits are in place. They rarely affect ordinary browsing, but they do shape how automated clients should behave.

Fair-use rate limiting

Requests are limited to roughly {{ rate_per_min }} per minute per IP address on the chart and API endpoints. Clients that exceed this receive an HTTP 429 (Too Many Requests) response with a Retry-After header; persistent offenders are blocked for a short period.

If you are polling the API, once every few hours is plenty — forecasts only refresh a handful of times a day. Please cache responses your end and avoid fetching every region and option combination in a tight loop.

Caching & data freshness

To reduce load, chart pages and API responses are served from a server-side cache that is regenerated whenever the underlying data actually changes — when a new forecast or fresh Agile prices arrive (a handful of times a day), or when the current half-hour pricing slot rolls over. Your browser may additionally cache a response for up to {{ cache_client_ttl }} seconds. In practice, what you see is never more than a few minutes behind the newest available data.

Automated access & AI crawlers

Bulk AI training and scraping crawlers (for example GPTBot, ClaudeBot, CCBot, PerplexityBot and similar) are disallowed in robots.txt. Such crawlers sweep every page and option combination, which generates far more load than human visitors and can make the site unavailable for real users.

Ordinary search-engine indexing and on-demand fetches (such as a person asking an assistant to open a specific page) are not blocked. Genuine integrations are welcome — please just use the API sensibly and within the rate limits above.

Traffic from IP ranges on a curated security blocklist (known-malicious sources) is also refused. If you believe your address is blocked in error, please get in touch.

Availability

Availability is best-effort. The service may be briefly slow or unavailable during traffic spikes, upstream data outages, or deployments. No warranty is given as to availability, accuracy or fitness for any purpose — see the notices in the footer.
